# SP-2100 and TL-2200

- Module: `instruments/sp2100_logger.js`
- View: custom
- TL-2200 serial: 38400, 8-N-1
- SP-2100 serial: user-selectable 38400/19200/9600/57600, default 38400, 8-N-1
- Poll: TL-2200 uses `R\r` every 300 ms; SP-2100 is passive and transmits a proprietary ComLink frame after a test
- Receive: both `onLine` and raw-byte `onByte`

Read `docs/SP2100_PROTOCOL_REFERENCE.md` completely before parser or waveform work. Also read the relevant section of `docs/INSTRUMENT_COMMUNICATION_PROTOCOLS.md`.

## Invariants

- Select baud rate from the model before connecting.
- Accept SP-2100 measurements only by the documented AVG/PEAK label rules.
- Accept TL-2200 records only in the documented token/CSV form.
- Do not replace label-based parsing with a generic numeric-field count.
- Preserve the 400 ms frame boundary, waveform candidate decoding, and PEAK-assisted selection unless real-device evidence supports a change.
- Preserve per-record waveform snapshots when changing tables or plots.
- Real-device audit on 2026-09-03 captured 3.4–3.7 KB proprietary binary blocks from COM5 at 38400 baud, while 57600 produced no response for this unit. Direct 8/16-bit candidate decoding was noise. Do not display or compute drag statistics from these bytes until IMASS DataLink/ExLink protocol or a matched vendor export establishes the conversion. See `docs/SP2100_RAW_CAPTURE_ANALYSIS_20260903.md`.

# LT-1000 Loop Tack

- Module: `instruments/lt1000_loop_tack.js`
- View: custom
- Serial: 9600, 8-N-1, no flow control
- Device sends data through its Print/Enter workflow.

Read the LT section of `docs/INSTRUMENT_COMMUNICATION_PROTOCOLS.md` before parsing changes.

Preserve multiline buffering, header/curve separation, zero-run detection, device peak handling, group numbering, outlier presentation, and CSV export. Do not treat arbitrary numeric lines as complete tests. Clear end-of-test timers and receive buffers on disconnect.

# PST-3202

- Module: `instruments/pst3202.js`
- View: custom
- Serial: 9600, 8-N-1, SCPI with LF termination
- Device: three-channel programmable DC supply

Read `docs/manuals/PST-3202.md`, the PST section of `AGENTS.md`, and the current module before changing output or protection behavior.

## Safety invariants

- Respect CH1/CH2 32 V, 2 A and CH3 6 V, 5 A physical limits.
- Preserve the stricter 2 A CH3 limit during three-channel synchronization.
- Keep independent synchronization distinct from physical parallel/series tracking.
- Do not enable output, change tracking, clear protection, or alter OVP/OCP behavior without tracing the full SCPI sequence and UI state.
- In series mode, combine voltage and keep shared current semantics for graphs and exports.
- Stop polling and cycles on disconnect; do not poll while output is off unless explicitly required.
- Preserve `/api/evaldata` persistence and `/api/export` behavior in development and EXE launchers.
